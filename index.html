Tactical Arsenal

Master the 4 Game-Changing Boosters

Turn impossible board situations into triumphant game-winning clears. Earn power-ups through gameplay or optional rewarded video milestones!

🔨

Hammer

Single Block Breaker

Pinpoint precision. Tap any single filled block on the board to smash it into dust, instantly creating room for your next piece.

Best Use: Removing isolated blocks that prevent a 3×3 square placement.
⚡

Line Clear

Dual Crosshair Blast

Electrifying power! Tap any cell on the board to immediately vaporize its entire horizontal row AND vertical column simultaneously.

Best Use: Clearing clogged intersections to reset critical center lanes.
🔄

Tray Swap

Fresh Shape Refresh

Instant mulligan. Tap to instantly discard all 3 current tray pieces and roll 3 brand-new polyomino shapes without ending your run.

Best Use: When none of the 3 tray pieces can fit onto the current board.
💣

Mega Bomb

3×3 Area Explosion

Maximum destruction. Target any cell to trigger a massive 3×3 area-of-effect blast, clearing up to 9 stubborn blocks at once.

Best Use: Emergency board rescue when dense clusters threaten game over.
Gameplay Guide

How to Play in 4 Simple Steps

Simple to learn in 30 seconds, endless depth to master over hundreds of games.

01
👆

Drag & Drop

Pick up polyomino shapes from the bottom tray and drag them onto the 10×10 grid board.

02
🔲

Complete Lines

Fill any full horizontal row or vertical column. Completed lines clear instantly, freeing up grid space.

03
💥

Build Multipliers

Clear 2 or more lines simultaneously to trigger score bonuses and chain combo multipliers for record high scores.

04
🎁

Deploy Boosters

When the board gets tight, tap in-game power-up buttons to use the Hammer, Line Clear, Swap, or Mega Bomb!
